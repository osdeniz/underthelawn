# Store copy — Under The Lawn

The review (G15) named the one thing no other game sells: **mowing is
discovering**. Tall grass is fog; every strip you cut shows you the world.
Powerwash Simulator sold *cleaning = satisfaction*; this sells
*cleaning = understanding*. Everything below is written from that sentence.

## One line

> Every mystery is under the grass.

TR: **Her sır çimenin altında.**

## Short description (store card, ≤ 80 words)

A girl is missing. The town has one detective and four lawnmowers.
Cut the grass and the yard tells you what happened here — a laced boot,
nine candles, two sets of prints with the small ones in front. Read the
evidence, follow the dog, bring her home. Then find out what she saw.

TR: Bir kız kayıp. Kasabanın bir dedektifi ve dört çim biçme makinesi var.
Çimi biç; bahçe sana burada ne olduğunu anlatır — bağcıklı bir bot, dokuz mum,
küçükleri önde iki çift ayak izi. Kanıtı oku, köpeği izle, onu eve götür.
Sonra ne gördüğünü öğren.

## Bullets

- Mow to reveal: the lawn is the map, the cut edge is the frontier.
- Three cases, twenty-seven chapters, a prologue on the long road.
- Evidence written to make you do the last step yourself.
- A dog that stops where something is buried. Watch him.
- Repair the town, feed the people who arrive. None of it is yours.
- One hand, portrait, two to four minutes a chapter. Pauses when you do.

## Pricing (decided G16.6)

Premium. Free: prologue + Case 01 chapters 1–3. One card after ch03, once.
No ads, no consumables, no timers.

## Do not say

The theme (care without possession) is never written down, in the game or
in the store. Do not use: "wholesome", "cozy", "zombie", "survival",
"post-apocalyptic". The world is quiet, not dead.
